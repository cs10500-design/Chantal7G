• Famous Artists and Artworks
  • Van Gogh's Success: Despite creating over 2,000 artworks, Vincent van Gogh only sold one painting during his lifetime.
  • Da Vinci's Patience: Leonardo da Vinci spent 12 years painting the lips of the Mona Lisa.
  • Picasso's Talent: Pablo Picasso could draw before he could walk.
  • Michelangelo's Signature: Michelangelo only signed one work, The Pieta, later viewing the act as too prideful.
